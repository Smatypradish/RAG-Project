CIRCULAR NO: COE/2026/FALL/003
Date: August 5, 2026
Authority: Controller of Examinations

Subject: Fall Semester 2026-2027 — Examination Schedule and Important Dates

This circular is issued to inform all students and faculty about the examination schedule and important deadlines for the Fall Semester 2026-2027.

1. CONTINUOUS ASSESSMENT TESTS

CAT-1 Schedule:
- Dates: September 22 to September 26, 2026
- Duration: 1 hour 30 minutes per exam
- Syllabus: As specified by respective course instructors

CAT-2 Schedule:
- Dates: November 3 to November 7, 2026
- Duration: 1 hour 30 minutes per exam
- Syllabus: As specified by respective course instructors

2. FINAL ASSESSMENT TEST (FAT)

FAT Schedule:
- Dates: December 8 to December 19, 2026
- Duration: 3 hours per exam
- Venue: As per seating arrangement published 3 days before exams

3. IMPORTANT DEADLINES

a) Exam Registration Deadline: September 15, 2026
   (As per Revised Academic Regulations 2026-2027)
   Note: No late registrations will be accepted.

b) Digital Assignment Submission: November 15, 2026
   All digital assignments must be submitted through the VTOP portal.

c) Revaluation Application Deadline: Within 15 days of result publication.
   Fee: Rs. 750 per subject.

d) Supplementary Exam Registration: January 5, 2027
   Maximum 6 subjects allowed.

4. EXAMINATION RULES

4.1 Students must carry their hall ticket and university ID card to every examination.

4.2 Mobile phones and electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall.

4.3 Students arriving more than 30 minutes late will not be permitted to enter the examination hall.

4.4 Any form of malpractice will result in cancellation of the examination and disciplinary action as per university rules.

5. RESULT PUBLICATION

Results will be published on the VTOP portal within 20 working days of the completion of the FAT examination.

Students with grievances regarding their results must contact the Controller of Examinations office within 7 working days of result publication.
