VELLORE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
REVISED ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2026-2027
(Version 2.0 — Supersedes Academic Regulations 2024-2025 v1.0)

Effective Date: July 1, 2026
Issued by: Office of the Vice Chancellor
Authority Level: University

REVISION NOTE: This document supersedes the Academic Regulations 2024-2025 (Version 1.0). Key changes include updated examination deadlines, revised grading thresholds, and updated scholarship criteria.

SECTION 1: GENERAL GUIDELINES

1.1 These Academic Regulations shall govern all undergraduate and postgraduate programs offered by VIT University from the academic year 2026-2027.

1.2 Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 5.5 to continue in the program. Students falling below this threshold for two consecutive semesters will be placed on academic probation.

1.3 The medium of instruction for all programs is English.

SECTION 2: REGISTRATION

2.1 Course Registration
Students must complete course registration during the designated registration period at the beginning of each semester. Late registration will incur a penalty fee of Rs. 1000 per day (revised from Rs. 500).

2.2 Minimum and Maximum Credits
- Undergraduate students: Minimum 16 credits, Maximum 25 credits per semester (revised from 27)
- Postgraduate students: Minimum 12 credits, Maximum 22 credits per semester (revised from 24)

2.3 Course Add/Drop
Students may add or drop courses within the first three weeks of the semester without any penalty (extended from two weeks). After the add/drop period, dropping a course will result in a "W" grade on the transcript.

SECTION 3: EXAMINATION

3.1 Continuous Assessment
Each course shall have continuous assessment components including:
- CAT-1 (Continuous Assessment Test 1): 15% weightage
- CAT-2 (Continuous Assessment Test 2): 15% weightage
- Digital Assignment: 10% weightage
- FAT (Final Assessment Test): 40% weightage
- Attendance and Participation: 5% weightage
- Quiz/Lab: 15% weightage

3.2 Exam Registration Deadline
Students must register for final examinations by September 15 of the Fall semester and by February 15 of the Winter semester. This deadline has been extended from the previous September 10 and February 10 deadlines. Late registration will not be accepted under any circumstances.

3.3 Revaluation
Students may apply for revaluation of their FAT answer scripts within 15 days of the publication of results (extended from 10 days). The revaluation fee is Rs. 750 per subject (revised from Rs. 500).

3.4 Supplementary Examinations
Students who fail in any subject may appear for the supplementary examination conducted at the beginning of the next semester. A maximum of 6 subjects can be registered for supplementary examination (increased from 5).

SECTION 4: GRADING SYSTEM

4.1 Grade Points
- S: 10 (91-100%)
- A: 9 (81-90%)
- B: 8 (71-80%)
- C: 7 (61-70%)
- D: 6 (51-60%)
- E: 5 (41-50%)
- F: 0 (Below 41%) - Fail
- N: 0 - Absent

4.2 CGPA Calculation
CGPA = Sum of (Grade Point x Credit) / Sum of Credits

4.3 Class/Division
- CGPA >= 9.0: First Class with Distinction (revised from 8.5)
- CGPA >= 7.0: First Class (revised from 6.5)
- CGPA >= 5.5: Second Class (revised from 5.0)

SECTION 5: ATTENDANCE

5.1 Minimum attendance required is 80% in each course (revised from 75%).

5.2 Students with attendance below 80% but above 70% may be given a chance to write the final examination with a penalty of 5 marks deduction.

5.3 Students with attendance below 70% are not eligible to write the final examination and must re-register for the course.

SECTION 6: SCHOLARSHIP

6.1 Merit Scholarship
Students with CGPA above 9.0 are eligible for a merit scholarship covering 60% of tuition fees (revised from 50%).

6.2 Need-Based Scholarship
Students from economically weaker sections may apply for need-based scholarships. Applications must be submitted within 45 days of admission (extended from 30 days).

6.3 Scholarship Renewal
Merit scholarships are renewed annually based on maintaining a CGPA of 9.0 or above (revised from 8.5).

SECTION 7: CERTIFICATES

7.1 Bonafide Certificate
Students can apply for bonafide certificates through the student portal. Processing time is 2 working days (reduced from 3).

7.2 Transcript
Official transcripts can be requested through the Examination Section. Fee: Rs. 300 per copy (revised from Rs. 200). Processing time is 5 working days.

7.3 Transfer Certificate
Transfer certificates are issued only after completion of the no-dues process. Students must clear all dues before applying.

SECTION 8: HOSTEL RULES

8.1 All first-year students are required to stay in the university hostel.

8.2 Hostel curfew timings: 10:30 PM on weekdays, 11:30 PM on weekends (revised from 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM).

8.3 Overnight leave must be approved by the warden at least 48 hours in advance (revised from 24 hours).

8.4 Hostel fee must be paid at the beginning of each semester along with tuition fees.
