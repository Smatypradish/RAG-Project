VELLORE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2024-2025
(Version 1.0)

Effective Date: July 1, 2024
Issued by: Office of the Vice Chancellor
Authority Level: University

SECTION 1: GENERAL GUIDELINES

1.1 These Academic Regulations shall govern all undergraduate and postgraduate programs offered by VIT University from the academic year 2024-2025.

1.2 Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 5.0 to continue in the program. Students falling below this threshold for two consecutive semesters will be placed on academic probation.

1.3 The medium of instruction for all programs is English.

SECTION 2: REGISTRATION

2.1 Course Registration
Students must complete course registration during the designated registration period at the beginning of each semester. Late registration will incur a penalty fee of Rs. 500 per day.

2.2 Minimum and Maximum Credits
- Undergraduate students: Minimum 16 credits, Maximum 27 credits per semester
- Postgraduate students: Minimum 12 credits, Maximum 24 credits per semester

2.3 Course Add/Drop
Students may add or drop courses within the first two weeks of the semester without any penalty. After the add/drop period, dropping a course will result in a "W" grade on the transcript.

SECTION 3: EXAMINATION

3.1 Continuous Assessment
Each course shall have continuous assessment components including:
- CAT-1 (Continuous Assessment Test 1): 15% weightage
- CAT-2 (Continuous Assessment Test 2): 15% weightage
- Digital Assignment: 10% weightage
- FAT (Final Assessment Test): 40% weightage
- Attendance and Participation: 5% weightage
- Quiz/Lab: 15% weightage

3.2 Exam Registration Deadline
Students must register for final examinations by September 10 of the Fall semester and by February 10 of the Winter semester. Late registration will not be accepted.

3.3 Revaluation
Students may apply for revaluation of their FAT answer scripts within 10 days of the publication of results. The revaluation fee is Rs. 500 per subject.

3.4 Supplementary Examinations
Students who fail in any subject may appear for the supplementary examination conducted at the beginning of the next semester. A maximum of 5 subjects can be registered for supplementary examination.

SECTION 4: GRADING SYSTEM

4.1 Grade Points
- S: 10 (91-100%)
- A: 9 (81-90%)
- B: 8 (71-80%)
- C: 7 (61-70%)
- D: 6 (51-60%)
- E: 5 (41-50%)
- F: 0 (Below 41%) - Fail
- N: 0 - Absent

4.2 CGPA Calculation
CGPA = Sum of (Grade Point x Credit) / Sum of Credits

4.3 Class/Division
- CGPA >= 8.5: First Class with Distinction
- CGPA >= 6.5: First Class
- CGPA >= 5.0: Second Class

SECTION 5: ATTENDANCE

5.1 Minimum attendance required is 75% in each course.

5.2 Students with attendance below 75% but above 65% may be given a chance to write the final examination with a penalty of 5 marks deduction.

5.3 Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible to write the final examination and must re-register for the course.

SECTION 6: SCHOLARSHIP

6.1 Merit Scholarship
Students with CGPA above 9.0 are eligible for a merit scholarship covering 50% of tuition fees.

6.2 Need-Based Scholarship
Students from economically weaker sections may apply for need-based scholarships. Applications must be submitted within 30 days of admission.

6.3 Scholarship Renewal
Merit scholarships are renewed annually based on maintaining a CGPA of 8.5 or above.

SECTION 7: CERTIFICATES

7.1 Bonafide Certificate
Students can apply for bonafide certificates through the student portal. Processing time is 3 working days.

7.2 Transcript
Official transcripts can be requested through the Examination Section. Fee: Rs. 200 per copy. Processing time is 5 working days.

7.3 Transfer Certificate
Transfer certificates are issued only after completion of the no-dues process. Students must clear all dues before applying.

SECTION 8: HOSTEL RULES

8.1 All first-year students are required to stay in the university hostel.

8.2 Hostel curfew timings: 10:00 PM on weekdays, 11:00 PM on weekends.

8.3 Overnight leave must be approved by the warden at least 24 hours in advance.

8.4 Hostel fee must be paid at the beginning of each semester along with tuition fees.
